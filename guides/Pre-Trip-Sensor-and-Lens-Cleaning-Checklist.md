**Cameras:** Panasonic Lumix G9 MkI, G9 MkII and GoPro HERO12 Black  
**Purpose:** remove the spots currently visible, clean every travel lens, GoPro and binocular, and leave objective before/after evidence before packing.

## 1. Safety boundary

- [ ] Work indoors on a cleared table, with doors/windows closed and fan or air conditioning off long enough for airborne dust to settle.
- [ ] Wash and dry hands. Keep caps, a clean microfiber cloth and the **Giottos manual blower** within reach.
- [ ] Use only a **manual camera blower** around the open mount. Never use canned/compressed air, a compressor, vacuum, breath, brush, cotton swab or lens pen on the sensor.
- [ ] Never insert the blower tip past the lens mount and never let it touch the sensor/shutter assembly.
- [ ] The owner-cleaning path ends after the camera cleaning cycle and careful blower treatment. If a repeatable spot persists, close the body and use professional service; this runbook does not authorize wet sensor cleaning.
- [ ] Do not run `Pixel Refresh` for dust. It is for persistent pixel defects, not particles.
- [ ] Put cleaning fluid, if needed for lens glass, on a clean lens tissue or cloth—never directly on the lens or camera.
- [ ] Use no solvent or liquid on lens/body electrical contacts. Remove loose material with a clean, dry, lint-free cloth only; do not use a pencil eraser.
- [ ] Use the **Ritz Gear Lens Cleaning Solution** only on accessible lens/filter glass in this workflow. Apply a minimal amount to the tissue/cloth, never directly to the equipment.
- [ ] The **CamKix Optical Lens Cleaner supplied with the APS-C sensor kit may be marketed for sensor use with that kit**, but it is not used on either G9 sensor in this Panasonic-aligned workflow.
- [ ] Do not mix Ritz Gear and CamKix or put them sequentially on the same cloth. Use a fresh tissue/clean cloth area when changing products.

Panasonic's official guidance limits owner sensor contact to a commercial blower, without touching the sensor, and recommends service if blowing does not remove the contamination. Its lens guidance is to blow loose particles away before gently wiping the glass with a soft dry cloth. References:

## 2. Equipment and evidence setup

- [ ] Fully charge one battery for each body; have both body caps ready and already dust-free.
- [ ] Inspect the Giottos blower and nozzle for damage or loose material. Hold it away from all equipment and squeeze it several times before first use to expel any stored particles.
- [ ] Never detach or fit the blower nozzle over an open camera, and never use the blower to suck material from a dirty surface.
- [ ] Inventory the rest of the cleaning kit by brand and contents before use: cloths, tissues, brushes and swabs must be identified separately. The known liquids are Ritz Gear Lens Cleaning Solution and CamKix Optical Lens Cleaner.
- [ ] Prepare for the default workflow: Giottos blower, Ritz Gear lens solution, at least two clean optical microfiber cloths, disposable lens tissues, small clean flashlight and clean resealable bags. Keep all sensor swabs and sensor liquids separately bagged and outside the work area.
- [ ] Treat unidentified liquid, brushes or swabs as **not approved for the sensor**. Sensor swabs remain outside this owner-cleaning workflow even if included in the kit.
- [ ] **Quarantine the available CamKix APS-C 16 mm sensor swabs: do not use them on either G9.** Both bodies are Micro Four Thirds, while these swabs are explicitly made for APS-C. Swab sizing is manufacturer-specific, so width alone cannot establish compatibility.
- [ ] Record the **Photographic Solutions Sensor Swab Ultra Type 2 / 17 mm** package condition and expiration date. The package contains 12 individually sealed swabs. Photographic Solutions identifies Type 2 as the correct size for the DC-G9 and Micro Four Thirds, so it is size-compatible with both bodies.
- [ ] Do not pair the Photographic Solutions swab with Ritz Gear or CamKix liquid. Its manufacturer specifies only **Eclipse** or **Aeroclipse** optic cleaning fluid; no compatible Photographic Solutions liquid is currently recorded in the kit.
- [ ] Keep the Type 2 swabs sealed and quarantined. Their correct size does not override Panasonic's blower-only owner guidance in this runbook.
- [ ] Do not use a microfiber cloth that has been used with skin oil, household cleaner or an unknown product.
- [ ] Create an evidence folder named `YYYY-MM-DD-camera-cleaning` outside the memory cards.
- [ ] In that folder, create `before`, `after` and `lens-tests` subfolders.
- [ ] Keep the original RAW files. JPEG previews may be exported for easy inspection, but do not replace the RAW evidence.

## 3. Standard sensor-spot test

Use the clean 12–35mm II as the reference lens on both bodies. Do not judge sensor dust by changing lenses between the before and after photographs.

1. Fit the 12–35mm II at `35 mm` and remove any front filter for the test.
2. Select a temporary `A` or `M` state; do not overwrite a Custom Mode.
3. Set `RAW`, `ISO 200`, `f/16`, mechanical shutter and manual focus deliberately far away from the target's focus plane.
4. Fill the frame with a uniformly illuminated matte white wall, overcast sky or clean diffused light panel. Do not point at the sun.
5. If the surface has texture, move the camera slightly during the exposure so the texture blurs while fixed sensor spots remain visible.
6. Capture two frames without changing the camera's orientation. Shift the framing laterally—or make a gentle pan during the second exposure—so target texture moves or blurs. A sensor spot remains at the same **image coordinates** in both files.
7. Inspect the entire frame at 100%, then temporarily increase contrast/Dehaze while inspecting. Do not bake those adjustments into the evidence RAW.
8. Record filenames and count/location of spots using image coordinates such as `upper-left`, `center-right` and `lower edge`.

Suggested names after import:

- `G9_SENSOR_BEFORE_01.RW2`, `G9_SENSOR_BEFORE_SHIFT_02.RW2`
- `G9II_SENSOR_BEFORE_01.RW2`, `G9II_SENSOR_BEFORE_SHIFT_02.RW2`
- Replace `BEFORE` with `AFTER` for the final pair.
- For the two-stage workflow, use `AFTER-BRAZIL` and `AFTER-USA` so no intermediate evidence is overwritten.

## 5. Clean all lenses before opening the bodies again

For every lens below, work from outside inward: clean barrel and caps first; with the lens pointed downward, use the blower on the front element; inspect under grazing light; only then wipe remaining fingerprints from the center outward with a clean optical cloth/tissue. Repeat on the rear element with a separate clean area of the cloth. Immediately recap both ends.

Do not chase harmless internal dust by disassembling a lens. Haze, fungus-like branching, oil, scratches, a loose element or contamination inside the lens goes to service.

### Outgoing 100–400mm — trade-in preparation

The currently owned 100–400mm is an outgoing trade-in item, not the incoming 100–400mm II travel lens. Keep the two records and accessories physically separated.

- [ ] Place the outgoing lens in a clearly labeled `TRADE-IN — DO NOT PACK` area with its own hood, front/rear caps, tripod collar/foot, pouch, box and documents that will accompany the evaluation.
- [ ] Before cleaning, photograph all sides under even light: full barrel, front rim, rear mount, switches, zoom/focus rings, tripod collar/foot, serial number, front element and rear element. Preserve these as the honest pre-cleaning condition record.
- [ ] With both caps fitted, use the Giottos blower and a clean soft brush to remove loose dust from barrel seams, switches, rings, hood, collar and foot. Never drive brush debris toward an open lens mount.
- [ ] Wipe the exterior with a separate clean, dry microfiber cloth. Do not apply Ritz Gear, CamKix, alcohol, polish, silicone, rubber dressing or household cleaner to paint, engraved markings, switches, seals or rubber rings.
- [ ] Extend/rotate the zoom only as required to expose normal exterior barrel surfaces; do not force the mechanism or attempt to clean inside gaps.
- [ ] Remove the front cap, blow the front element and use Ritz Gear on lens tissue/cloth only if a fingerprint or bonded mark remains. Never spray the lens directly.
- [ ] Recap the front, turn the lens mount downward, remove the rear cap, blow and clean the rear element only if needed, then recap immediately.
- [ ] Clean electrical contacts only with a dry, lint-free cloth. Do not use liquid or an eraser.
- [ ] Clean hood and both caps separately, ensuring no loose grit remains inside before refitting them.
- [ ] Test on a camera: recognition, AF, aperture reporting, O.I.S., zoom through the full normal range and one detailed frame at both ends. Record any real fault rather than repeatedly exercising it.
- [ ] After cleaning, repeat the same condition photographs and add two unedited test files. Keep before/after images and serial-number evidence until the trade-in is completed.
- [ ] Pack only when fully dry, capped and with its matching accessories. Do not include accessories intended for the incoming 100–400mm II.

## 6. Clean the two sensors

Complete one body from start to finish before opening the second.

### G9 MkI

- [ ] Fit a fully charged battery and run `Sensor Cleaning` from the camera menu with a lens fitted.
- [ ] Power the body off, wait several seconds, remove the battery, then remove the lens with the mount facing downward and keep the opening angled downward throughout.
- [ ] Without entering the mount, give a few controlled puffs with the Giottos blower. Keep the blower tip and hand clear of the mount and sensor.
- [ ] Refit the clean 12–35mm II promptly, reinstall the battery and power the camera on so its normal dust-reduction cycle runs.
- [ ] Capture and inspect the two `AFTER` test frames using exactly the baseline protocol.
- [ ] If the spots are gone, stop cleaning. Repeated unnecessary cleaning only adds risk.
- [ ] If a spot persists, repeat the menu cleaning and blower step only once. If still present, close the body and proceed to the service gate below.

### G9 MkII

- [ ] Repeat the same menu-cleaning, power-off, battery-removal, downward-mount blower, immediate lens-refit and battery-reinstallation sequence.
- [ ] Capture and inspect the two `AFTER` test frames using exactly the baseline protocol.
- [ ] If a spot persists after one repeated menu/blower cycle, close the body and proceed to the service gate below.

### Persistent-spot service gate

Professional cleaning is the Panasonic-aligned choice after blower cleaning fails. Complete these diagnostics before sending a body for service:

- [ ] The remaining mark is repeatable at the same image coordinates in both shifted-framing `f/16` test frames.
- [ ] Repeat one frame at `f/2.8`. A dust shadow should become much less distinct; a sharp bright/dark point that is essentially unchanged may be a pixel issue and is not a reason to swab.
- [ ] If identity is still uncertain, fit a second already-clean lens and repeat `f/16`. A mark that remains at the same image coordinates with both lenses supports sensor contamination; a mark that follows only one lens does not.
- [ ] Record the location and appearance of every remaining mark and attach the before/after RAW files.
- [ ] Bag and label the CamKix APS-C 16 mm swabs, Photographic Solutions Type 2 / 17 mm swabs and CamKix liquid separately; none is used on either G9 in this workflow.
- [ ] Arrange professional sensor cleaning and provide the diagnostic files. After service, repeat the full standard sensor test before accepting the body.

Do not wet-clean either sensor as part of this runbook, even if the MFT swab is the correct physical size. The stop condition is deliberate: Panasonic's owner procedure permits a blower without sensor contact and directs persistent contamination to service.

## 7. Final acceptance photographs for every lens

Use a body that has already passed its final sensor test. These images confirm clean external optics, normal aperture/AF communication and absence of obvious new marks; the reference-lens test remains the authoritative sensor test.

For each lens, take one evenly lit plain-field frame at `f/11` and one detailed real-scene frame at `f/8`. Focus normally for the detailed frame. For zooms, take the detailed frame at both ends of the zoom range. Review at 100% for spots, smear, flare-like streaks, uneven softness and exposure/aperture errors.

- [ ] Leica 9mm f/1.7: plain field; detailed scene at f/8; verify AF, aperture reporting and the clean 55 mm filter/hood fit.
- [ ] 12–35mm II: plain field; detailed scene at 12mm and 35mm.
- [ ] 35–100mm II: plain field; detailed scene at 35mm and 100mm.
- [ ] 100–400mm II: plain field; detailed scene at 100mm and 400mm; verify O.I.S., AF and aperture reporting.
- [ ] Olympus 60mm Macro: plain field; detailed normal-distance frame and a close-focus/macro frame.
- [ ] 100–400mm II + DMW-TC14: recognition, reported aperture, AF and one detailed frame at a supported focal length.
- [ ] 100–400mm II + DMW-TC20: recognition, reported aperture, AF and one detailed frame at a supported focal length.
- [ ] Macro lens + Raynox DCR-250: secure fit and one close-focus frame without touching the subject or diffuser.
- [ ] GoPro HERO12: inspect a plain bright frame and a detailed 4K frame for smears, flare and damage to the lens cover; this is an optical check, not a requirement to validate the configured modes.
- [ ] Zeiss Victory SF 8x32: inspect a distant detailed target with each eye separately and then binocularly; confirm clean optics, comfortable collimation and normal focus/diopter operation.
